    Method

    The champion rule, in the organiser's words:

    “Person with highest cumulative stableford points over six or less games wins at end of comp. Add this so best 6 max stableford scores determine champ.”

    Points. Stableford points as recorded in Golf GameBook: net, off each player's handicap on the day. Higher is better throughout this report.

    Ties. Players level on total share the place, shown with a T (T2, T2, then 4).

    Source. Every figure on this page is calculated from one file, data-winter-2027.js. Nothing is typed into the page by hand.
